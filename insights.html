What the family labels mean

Family is who wrote the text we sing. A later cover or arrangement does not move the song. These labels are for diet, not for grade.

Is He Worthy stays Modern hymn (Peterson), even when Shane & Shane arranged it. Behold the Lamb (Passion) stays Modern. The Getty communion hymn is Getty.
Modern
Contemporary congregational songs from the 1990s onward that do not belong to a named house below. Tomlin, Redman, Wickham, Passion writers, and similar CCLI staples sit here.
Hymn
Texts that entered the church’s hymnody before the modern worship industry. Watts, Wesley, Crosby, traditional Irish and English sources, and the usual Sunday hymnal core.
Sovereign Grace
Songs written for Sovereign Grace Music. Psalm settings from that house stay here even when another band recorded them.
Chorus
Short 1980s–90s congregational choruses. Maranatha, Integrity, Vineyard-adjacent communion pieces, and similar one-page texts fold into this line.
Modern hymn
Recent texts written in hymn form or with that density: Boswell/Papa, Keyes, Peterson, Townend-adjacent pieces that are not Getty or CityAlight catalog. Cover versions stay in this family.
Vineyard
Songs first written in the Vineyard stream. Includes May the Words, Holy Holy Holy (Vineyard), Make Us a Prayer, and the other Vineyard titles in our list.
CityAlight
Songs written by the CityAlight team (Robinson, Thompson, and co-writers on those texts).
Getty
Keith and Kristyn Getty texts, including Townend co-writes published as Getty songs (In Christ Alone, Speak O Lord, The Power of the Cross).
Hymn / Christmas
Classic nativity and incarnation hymns we keep on the Christmas setlists. Same rule as Hymn; tagged so the diet chart can separate seasonal use.
Shane & Shane / Worship Initiative
Texts written in the Worship Initiative circle and published that way. In this catalog: Abide, All Sufficient Merit, and Psalm 90 (Satisfy Us with Your Love). Arrangements of other writers do not land here.
Chorus medley
Two short choruses we treat as one setlist row (As We Seek / Jesus Draw Me Close; The Steadfast Love of the Lord / As We Gather).
Built from BCC Song List V2 setlists, 2023 through Sept 2026. Full lyrics stay off these pages.
