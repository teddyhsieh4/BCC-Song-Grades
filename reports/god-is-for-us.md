## Thesis

The congregation will not fear the battle or the night. They will walk the valley with the Lord by their side. The Father’s love is a strong and mighty fortress. Who can stand against them if God is for them? Even when the singer stumbles, falls, or turns back, the Lord’s love is sure. He will not abandon or forsake. Neither height nor depth can separate. Hell and death will not defeat. He who gave His Son to free us holds the singer in His love.

---

## Overall recommendation

Strong

Use it regularly, and keep the bridge. It carries Romans 8: the Father gave His Son to free us, His love holds us, and nothing can separate us from it. The rest of the lyric confesses that God is for His people and that His love stays sure when they stumble. The church has sung it 7 times across at least four years of the 2023–2026 window.

## Voice and addressee

Mostly first-person plural, with first-person singular in verse 2 and the bridge. It mixes declaration and testimony, and the whole congregation can own both.

---

## How the text moves

Verse 1 is a corporate confession of courage under God’s leading, and the chorus answers with Romans 8:31: who can stand against us if God is for us. Verse 2 brings that home to the one who stumbles and turns back. The bridge gives the reason, naming the Son who was given and the love nothing can break.

---

## Lines you cannot cut

“He who gave His son to free us / Holds me in His love” and “Neither height nor depth can separate us.”

Without these lines the song still says God is for us, but it no longer says why. The giving of the Son and the love nothing can break are in the bridge and nowhere else.

## Persons of the Trinity named

The Father is named, and the Son is named as the one given to free us. The Spirit is not named.

---

## Could another worshiper sing this unchanged?

No. A vague theist would have to change the Son given to free us, which is the reason the song gives for God being for us.

---

- **Unbiblical:** none.

## Theological Accuracy and Biblical Fidelity: 5

“Our God is for us” and “Who can stand against us if our God is for us” are Romans 8:31. “The Father’s love is a strong and mighty fortress” draws on Psalm 18:2 and Proverbs 18:10. “You will not abandon, You will not forsake” is Deuteronomy 31:6 and Hebrews 13:5. “Neither height nor depth can separate us / Hell and death will not defeat us / He who gave His son to free us / Holds me in His love” is Romans 8:38–39 and Romans 8:32. Every line is accurate to its text.

---

## Christ-Exalting and Gospel-Saturated: 4

The Son is named as the one the Father gave to free us (Romans 8:32), and the bridge says nothing can separate us from that love. The score is 4 and not 5 because the cross, substitution, and resurrection are never named. It is 4 and not 3 because the bridge does name the giving of the Son.

---

## Objectivity vs. Subjectivity: 4

The chorus and verse 1 declare what God is and will do, and the bridge declares Romans 8. Verse 2 is testimony, and it rests on God’s sure love rather than on anything the singer promises. That testimony is why the score is 4 and not 5.

---

## Description of God’s Character: 4

God is for His people, His love is a fortress, He will not forsake, and He gave His Son to free us. Each attribute is specific. The score is 4 because the lyric stays on His love and faithfulness and does not name holiness or justice.

---

## Congregational Usefulness and Clarity: 5

The words are plain and the chorus is easy to learn. The 5 comes from the bridge: a congregation that sings it learns Romans 8:32–39, that the Father who gave His Son will never let go.

---

## Tone

Joyful and confident, the boldness of Romans 8:31 rather than bravado.

---

## What the congregation never says

The congregation never names the Spirit, the cross, or the resurrection. When the service needs the finished work spelled out, pair it with In Christ Alone.

---

## Setlist notes

- **When it serves the sermon.** Romans 8, Psalm 18, Deuteronomy 31, Hebrews 13, or any text on God being for His people and His love that will not let go.
- **Gaps the rest of the hour must cover.** The cross and the empty tomb, if the service has not already named them.
- **Diet sentence.** Adds a clear confession that God is for His people, that His love holds through stumbling, and that the Father who gave His Son will not let them be separated from Him. It does not add the cross or the resurrection.
- **What not to do.** Do not drop the bridge and sing only the verses and chorus.
