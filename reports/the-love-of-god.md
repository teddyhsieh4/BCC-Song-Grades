## Thesis

The love of God is greater than any tongue or pen can tell. It reaches beyond the highest star and to the lowest hell. God gave His Son to win the guilty pair, reconciled the erring child, and pardoned from sin. That love will still endure when earthly kingdoms fall, and it is redeeming grace to Adam’s race.

---

## Overall recommendation

Excellent / Prefer

Feature it, and keep the first stanza: it is where God gives His Son to win the guilty, reconciles the erring child, and pardons sin. The refrain and the later stanzas then sing how great and lasting that love is. The church has sung it 8 times across at least four years of the 2023–2026 window.

## Voice and addressee

Third-person declaration about the love of God, with the refrain addressed to that love (“O love of God”). The congregation sings objective claims and makes no vows.

---

## How the text moves

The first stanza shows the love at work: God gave His Son to win and pardon the guilty. The second looks to the end of the world, when that love still stands. The third says no amount of ink or parchment could ever write it all down.

---

## Lines you cannot cut

“The guilty pair, bowed down with care, / God gave His Son to win; / His erring child He reconciled, / And pardoned from his sin.”

Without the first stanza, the hymn praises how great the love is without showing what it did.

## Persons of the Trinity named

God is named. His Son is named. The Spirit is not named. The love is God’s, shown by the Son given.

---

## Could another worshiper sing this unchanged?

No. A vague theist could not sing “God gave His Son to win” the guilty pair without changing it, and the third stanza’s praise of the love points back to that gift.

---

## Unbiblical, non-biblical, thin

- **Unbiblical:** none.
- **Non-biblical:** the third stanza’s image of ink, parchment, quills, and scroll is poetry that no single text supports, though its claim (the love is greater than can be told) is biblical.
- **Thin:** none that matter.

---

## Theological Accuracy and Biblical Fidelity: 5

“The guilty pair, bowed down with care, / God gave His Son to win” is John 3:16 and Romans 5:8. “His erring child He reconciled, / And pardoned from his sin” is 2 Corinthians 5:18–19 and Ephesians 1:7. “It goes beyond the highest star, / And reaches to the lowest hell” is Romans 8:38–39 and Psalm 139:8. “Redeeming grace to Adam’s race” is Romans 5:15–19. The third stanza’s image is older poetry that serves the same claim of immeasurable love (Ephesians 3:18–19).

---

## Christ-Exalting and Gospel-Saturated: 5

The first stanza names the Son given to win the guilty, the reconciliation of the erring child, and the pardon of sin. The love is shown by what it did.

---

## Objectivity vs. Subjectivity: 5

The lyric declares what God’s love is and what God has done. It reports no feelings and makes no vows. The refrain calls the love rich, pure, measureless, and strong.

---

## Description of God’s Character: 4

God’s love is rich, pure, measureless, strong, and lasting, and it reaches from the highest star to the lowest hell. Holiness and justice appear mainly in the lowest hell and in those who refuse to pray. The score is 4 because the hymn is about one attribute.

---

## Congregational Usefulness and Clarity: 5

The congregation already knows the third stanza well. The first stanza teaches the Son given and the sinner pardoned, so keep it with the familiar parts.

---

## Tone

The tone is reverent and full of wonder at how great God’s love is.

---

## What the congregation never says

The congregation never names the resurrection, the Spirit, or Christ’s present intercession. When the sermon needs the resurrection, pair it with Christ Our Hope in Life and Death.

---

## Setlist notes

- **When it serves the sermon.** John 3, Romans 5, Romans 8:38–39, Ephesians 3:18–19, or any text on God’s love shown in the gift of His Son.
- **Gaps the rest of the hour must cover.** The resurrection, and the Spirit, if the service has not named them.
- **Diet sentence.** Adds a hymn on the size of God’s love, tied to the Son given and sin pardoned. It does not add the empty tomb.
- **What not to do.** Do not drop the first stanza, and do not sing the third stanza on its own.
