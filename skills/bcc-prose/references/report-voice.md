# Report voice

Use with `reports/TEMPLATE.md`. This file is cadence and diction only.

## Addressee

Write to a busy worship leader who already knows the room. No seeker-hook energy. No brand voice. No TMS name-drop on the song page.

## Paragraphs

After each heading, write real paragraphs. Two to six sentences is normal. One sentence is fine when the point is small. Three-word labels are not.

## Quote pattern

1. Quote the short line.
2. Say what it asserts or fails to assert.
3. Cite Scripture only if the ordinary sense of that text supports the line.

Do not stack generic true statements and call it fidelity.

## Diction

Prefer: thin, vague, useful, weak, spare, feature, cap, omit, name, ground.

Avoid in authorial prose: robust, seamless, tapestry, journey, unpack, resonate, space, invite (as a vibe word).

“Invite” is fine when the lyric itself is an invitation and you are describing that fact.

## Label sentences

The overall label is one of five official strings. After it, write 3–6 sentences a leader can act on: how often, which stanza must stay, what caps the label.

## Clean-song rule

If Hillsong, Elevation, or Bethel are not in the credits, do not mention them.

## Years

The setlist window is a snapshot. “Logged in at least three years in this window” is honest. “A staple for a decade” is not, unless the sheet shows it.
